When to switch players

Players only score on days their team plays. The planner swaps a player whose team is about to rest for one whose team has more games coming up, and picks the best day to make each trade.

Trades lock when the first game of a game day starts and reopen after that day's points are updated. A trade "before ti 17.11." must be made after the previous game day's points update and before the first faceoff on 17.11. Prices change daily, so check that each trade still fits your money.

Switch one player

Who to switch, and when

Each of your players has a card with its own slider. Move the slider to the day you'd make the trade. The two rows show who plays on which day: your current player until the trade, then the best replacement after it. The card tells you exactly what to do and how many LPP you gain. Cards are sorted so the best switch is first.
